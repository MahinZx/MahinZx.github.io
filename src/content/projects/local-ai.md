## Running models locally
Personal experiments with Ollama and models from the Qwen and Gemma families. The focus is understanding how local inference behaves on available hardware.

## Areas of exploration
- Model setup and configuration.
- Troubleshooting local inference.
- Observing responsiveness and hardware constraints.
- Comparing configurations through practical use.

## What this work represents
These are learning experiments, not published research or a production AI service. No benchmark scores or model-performance improvements are claimed.
